<div class="theorem-box">

**定理：ガウス積分**

$$
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty \e^{-ax^2} \dd x
=
\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{a}}
$$

</div>

<details class="proof-box">
<summary>証明</summary>

</details>

ガンマ関数の定義より、

$$
\qquad
\Gamma\qty(\dfrac12)
=
\dint_0^\infty
\dfrac{\e^{-t}}{\sqrt t}
\dd t
$$

であり、$t=ax^2$ と置換すると、

$$
\qquad
\phantom{\Gamma\qty(\dfrac12)}
=
\dint_0^\infty
\dfrac{\e^{-t}}{\sqrt t}
\dd t
$$

$$
\qquad
\phantom{=}
\left(
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\e^{-ax^2}
\dd x
\right)^2
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\e^{-ax^2}
\dd x
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\e^{-ay^2}
\dd y
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\e^{-ay^2-ay^2}
\dd x
\dd y
$$

ここで $x=r\cos\theta,\, y=r\sin\theta$ と置換すると、ヤコビアンは $r$ だから、

$$
\qquad
=
\dint_0^\infty
\dint_0^{2\pi}
\e^{-ar^2}
r
\dd\theta
\dd r
$$

$$
\qquad
=
2\pi
\dint_0^\infty
\e^{-ar^2}
r
\dd r
$$

$$
\qquad
=
2\pi
\left[
\dfrac{\e^{-ar^2}}{-2a}
\right]_0^\infty
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\dlim_{N \to \infty}
2\pi
\left[
\dfrac{\e^{-ar^2}}{-2a}
\right]_0^N
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\dlim_{N \to \infty}
2\pi
\left(
\dfrac{\e^{-aN^2}}{-2a}
-
\dfrac{\e^{-a \cdot 0^2}}{-2a}
\right)
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\pi
\left(
\dfrac{\dlim_{N \to \infty}\e^{-aN^2}}{-a}
+
\dfrac{\e^{-a \cdot 0^2}}{a}
\right)
$$

```latex
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \draw[<->,>=latex, semithick] (-4, 0)--(4, 0)node[right]{$N$}; %x軸
  \draw[<->,>=latex, semithick] (0, -1)--(0, 3)node[above]{$f(N)$}; %y軸
  \draw (0,0)node[below right]{O}; %原点
  \draw[semithick, smooth, samples=100, domain = -4:4]
    plot(\x, 2*exp(-\x*\x));
  \node at (2.5, 2.5) {$f(N)=\mathrm{e}^{-aN^2}$};
  \draw (-.1, 2) -- (.1, 2) node[thick, right] {$1$};
\end{tikzpicture}
```

$N \to \infty \ (\mathrm{e}^{-aN^2} \to 0)$ が成り立つから、

$$
\qquad
=
\pi
\left(
\dfrac{0}{-a}
+
\dfrac{1}{a}
\right)
$$

$$
\qquad
=
\dfrac{\pi}{a}.
$$

したがって、

$$
\qquad
\dint_{-\infty}^\infty
\e^{-ax^2}
\dd x
=
\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{a}}.
\ \Box
$$
