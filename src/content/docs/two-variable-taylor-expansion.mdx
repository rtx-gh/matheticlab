**定理：テイラーの定理**

$$
f(a+h,b+k)
=
\dsum_{n=0}^\infty
\dfrac1{n!}
\qty(
h\pdv x+k\pdv y
)^n
f(a,b)
+R_n
$$

$$
R_n
=
\dfrac1{n!}
\left(
h\dpdv x+k\dpdv y
\right)^n
f(a+\theta h,b+\theta k)
$$

f_{xx} & f_{xy} \\
f_{xy} & f_{yy}
\end{vmatrix}
$$

<div class="definition-box">

**定義：ヘッシアン**

$$
H
=
\begin{vmatrix}
f_{xx}(a,b) & f_{xy}(a,b) \\
f_{xy}(a,b) & f_{yy}(a,b)
\end{vmatrix}
=
f_{xx}(a,b)f_{yy}(a,b)-f_{xy}^2(a,b)
$$
